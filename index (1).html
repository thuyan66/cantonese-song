⌨️ Cantonese Typing Guide

Traditional characters and Jyutping (粵拼).

Jyutping uses letters and tone numbers 1–6: 你好 nei5 hou2. Install a Cantonese phonetic keyboard where available, or use Traditional Chinese handwriting, Cangjie or stroke input. On a computer, a Jyutping-compatible input method such as Rime can be used. Keyboard availability varies by device.

For the main typing games, enter Traditional Chinese characters, not Jyutping. In full dictation, spaces are optional.
